Pools every swing loaded and fits each outcome to the factors assumed to drive it, plus mob level. Each chart is the fitted model seen from above: the stat across, every mob level down, and color for the model's value with the other factors held at typical values. Hatched cells were never logged, so the color there is the model's guess. Dots mark what was actually logged; hover anything for its numbers. Factors that never change in the data can't be measured.
